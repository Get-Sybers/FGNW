This is what is probably the most accurate Naruto Timeline on the internet. I've been working on this timeline for the last couple of years, basing it mostly on the manga and the databooks, but also taking events from the anime and novels into account, if possible. It covers the entire Naruto history from the beginning of all chakra and Kaguya Ōtsutsuki to the Fourth Shinobi World War and beyond, even Boruto: Naruto Next Generations. It includes every (relevant) statement about when something took place, even more specific dates like weeks and days. I have also created two smaller timelines that deal with two of the most prominent errors: The life of Kakashi, Obito and Rin and the three Shinobi World Wars. These are linked below.
Please refrain from editing this page, but feel free to make improvements on the talk page. I especially have to thank Elveonora and ItachiWasAHero for contributing to the cause by making me aware of mistakes and providing information to add. I also have to thank Platypus @ NF for his very detailed list of dates from the Itachi Shinden novels. Furthermore, this timeline was inspired by my predecessor ShounenSuki's timeline.

Legend
bNb means before Naruto's birth.
aNb means after Naruto's birth.
Information written in italics have to be re-confirmed.
Striked information was retconned, as explained in the references.
At the earliest means that the date is the earliest possible date the event could've happened.
At the latest means that the date is the latest possible date the event could've happened.
In the fourth databook, dates are usually given in one of these three ways:
X年前 X-nen mae — translated as X years ago, reflected as "This year" in the timeline, if no further information was given.
約X年前 yaku X-nen mae — translated as About X years ago, reflected as "About this year" in the timeline, if no further information was given.
約X数年前 yaku X-sūnen mae — translated as About X0 to X9 years ago, not reflected in a special way.
Databooks
Most of the dates in this timeline come from the four databooks. Each of them covers a specific range of volumes and chapters; the Rin no Sho covers the manga up to volume 12 (chapter 108), the Tō no Sho covers the manga up to volume 27 (chapter 244), the Sha no Sho covers the manga up to volume 43 (chapter 402) and the Jin no Sho covers everything up to volume 71 (chapter 690) as well as chapter 691 from volume 72. It specifically ends on the 10th October.
For the first three databooks, it's unclear at which date they end. Furthermore, the characters are always made one year older with each new databook, which contradicts the dates provided in the manga itself. This is best explained by looking at Hiruzen's age: In the first databook, his age was stated to be 68. In the following databooks, his age at death is given as 69. However, he was born on 8th February and from the manga, we know that he died between July and September, meaning he could only have become 69 years old if there was a gap year between chapter 108 and 109, which isn't the case.
